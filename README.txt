DIPAK AHER — PERSONAL PORTFOLIO
================================

HOW TO OPEN ON YOUR MAC
1. Download and extract dipak-personal-portfolio.zip.
2. Open VS Code.
3. Choose File > Open Folder... and select the extracted `dipak-personal-portfolio` folder.
4. Open `index.html`.
5. To preview, right-click `index.html` and choose "Open with Live Server" if the Live Server extension is installed.
   Or double-click index.html in Finder to open it in your browser.

FILES
- index.html       Page content and sections
- style.css        Minimal dark theme, responsive layout, motion and styling
- script.js        Mobile menu, reveal animations, active navigation
- assets/dipak-photo.jpg  Your uploaded campus photo
